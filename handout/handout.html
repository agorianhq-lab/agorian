First time

1. Open agorian.au
Laptop or phone, no login, nothing to install. Press Let's go.
2. Do Today's ten
Ten questions aimed at what you find hardest. It changes every day and remembers what you got wrong.
3. Read the Before the exam page
What the paper looks like, how to spend the 50 minutes and where the marks are.
4. Pick a weak topic
Scroll to Your progress by topic. Anything under 70 per cent, tap it and practise.

What's there

Practice. Pick topics, answer at your pace, explanation after every question.
Challenge. 20 questions, 10 minutes, both units.
Timeline. Both units side by side, 800 to 1900. Starred dates are the ones to learn.
Key terms. Flashcards and a ten-definition sprint.
Short answers. 2, 3 and 4-mark questions with a full-marks and a part-marks answer to compare with yours.
Paragraph practice. Six exam-style prompts with a TEEAL scaffold and a timer for exam conditions.
Unseen sources. A source you have not seen, four quick questions, then a timed 3-mark answer.

A week that works

Every dayToday's ten (five minutes). Read every explanation, especially when you were right by luck.
Three timesOne Short answers question, written properly, then marked against the exemplars. Honest marks only.
TwiceOne paragraph under the 15-minute timer. Then read it back against the criteria on the paper.
OnceChallenge mode, to see how you cope with the clock. Then Key terms for anything you blanked on.
Last two daysYour progress by topic. Practise only the topics under 70 per cent. Stop revising what you already know.
Nothing is recorded. Your scores and drafts stay in the browser on the device you use and are never sent anywhere, so use the same device each time and don't clear your browser data before the exam. Agorian never asks for your name, an email address or a password. If a page does, it is not Agorian.
