Agorian logo concepts

Navy #243A6B and vermilion #C7431F from the revision deck palette. Each mark shown large, as a small app icon, and on dark.

A · The stoa

Agorian

The colonnaded hall that edged every agora, with the pediment in vermilion. Reads as both a Greek building and a capital A. Already in the site header.

B · The open square

Agorian

Four corner brackets frame an empty square, the agora itself, with the A standing in it. Works at any size and the brackets read as "focus" in a modern interface.

C · The assembly

Agorian

Eight citizens gathered in a ring around the speaker. The most literal telling of the agora story, and the dots can become students' progress markers in the interface.

D · The wordmark alone

Agorian

No symbol. The two-colour break at "ian" is the mark, echoing how a Greek root takes a suffix. The quietest option and the easiest to keep consistent everywhere. Falls back to a plain "A" for the browser tab.
